Share kit: har kaam 1 tap. Naam likho, button dabao, contact chuno, Send.

1. Apne logon ko message (sabse zaroori)

WhatsApp pe bhejo

Button dabane pe WhatsApp khulega, contact chuno aur Send. Link ke saath product ki photo apne aap dikhegi.

2. WhatsApp Status (din mein 3 baar)

"Status pe lagao" dabao, WhatsApp chuno, "My status". Agar share na khule to photo ko daba ke rakho, "Download image", phir Status pe lagao.

3. Instagram / Facebook post

Caption copy hoke clipboard mein aa jayega. Instagram mein photo chuno, caption box mein paste karo.

4. Order aa jaaye to

Admin kholo (Confirm on WhatsApp)

Koi website pe order na kar paaye: WhatsApp pe naam, pata, pincode, rang le lo aur khud website pe unke liye order daal do (Cash on Delivery).
